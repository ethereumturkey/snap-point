Snap Point

Same pile. Two builds. One snaps. Pick a side — physics decides.

Snap Point is a YouTube Shorts channel of short, stylized physics experiments: structures built from the same materials are put under load until one of them fails. Every outcome comes from a real physics simulation; results are never edited or chosen by hand.

Watch on YouTube: @SnapPointLab

The channel is operated with a small private tool ("Snap Point Engine") that uploads our own videos to our own YouTube channel and reads that channel's analytics. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
