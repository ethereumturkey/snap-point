Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02

Snap Point Engine ("the app") is a private tool used only by the operator of the Snap Point YouTube channel to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public and has no end users other than the channel operator.

What data the app accesses

When the channel operator signs in with Google, the app requests access to YouTube API Services to: upload and schedule videos to the operator's own channel; read the operator's own channel and video metadata; and read YouTube Analytics for the operator's own channel (for example views, watch time, likes and audience retention). The app also reads publicly available video statistics (such as view counts) of public YouTube channels for research.

How the data is used

Data is used only to publish the operator's videos and to measure which of the operator's videos perform better, so future videos can be improved. Data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to identify or profile any individual viewer. The app never receives personal information about viewers.

Some figures shown by the app (for example hook, completion and loop scores) are calculated by Snap Point Engine from YouTube data. They are our own metrics, they are labelled as such in the app, and they are not provided by YouTube.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens and API data are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a local database that is not shared. YouTube Analytics data and statistics of the operator's own videos are kept only as long as they are needed to compare the operator's videos, and the app checks at least every 30 days that its access to the channel has not been revoked. Other data about the operator's channel and videos is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Statistics of channels not owned by the operator, obtained through YouTube API Services, are deleted after at most 30 days. If access is revoked, all data obtained with that access is deleted as soon as possible and within 7 days.

Cookies and device storage

The app is a command-line program that runs only on the operator's own computer. It does not use cookies, web beacons or similar tracking technologies on any user's device. It stores its OAuth tokens and data only in local files on the operator's computer. We do not add cookies or analytics to this website.

Deleting your data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at Google security settings (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). To ask us to delete stored data, email seloselahattin@gmail.com. We delete it as soon as possible and within 7 days.

YouTube API Services and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at any time at Google security settings.
